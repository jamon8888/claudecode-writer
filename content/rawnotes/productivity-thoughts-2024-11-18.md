Just some random thoughts about productivity and remote work...

I've been noticing that time blocking doesn't really work for remote workers. Traditional productivity advice assumes you have stable energy throughout the day, but when you're working from home, your energy fluctuates based on so many factors - kids, pets, household tasks, etc.

Maybe we need to think about "energy blocking" instead - scheduling work around when you naturally have the most energy, rather than arbitrary time blocks.

Also, the whole idea of a "home office" isn't realistic for everyone. Not everyone has a spare room. But maybe we don't need a physical space - we need psychological transitions. Like a ritual that signals "work mode" vs "home mode".

Remote meetings are another thing. We're doing way too many of them. Just because we CAN meet virtually doesn't mean we SHOULD. Most meetings could be async updates.
