## Keyword Usage Guidelines

### Density Targets
- Primary keyword: 1.0-2.0% density
- Secondary keywords: 0.5-1.0% density
- Natural integration (no keyword stuffing)

### Placement Priority
1. **Title**: Include primary keyword near beginning
2. **H1**: Primary keyword (naturally)
3. **First Paragraph**: Primary keyword in first 100 words
4. **H2 Headers**: Secondary and related keywords
5. **Throughout Content**: Natural variations
6. **Conclusion**: Primary keyword (variation)
7. **Meta Description**: Primary keyword
8. **Image Alt Text**: Keywords where relevant

## Keyword Avoidance

*Keywords to avoid (off-brand, too competitive, wrong audience)*

- General "productivity" (too broad)
- "Work from home jobs" (not our focus)
- Tool-specific unless strategic (locks into vendor)
- Clickbait terms ("secrets", "hacks" unless genuinely valuable)
